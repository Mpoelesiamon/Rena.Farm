Our Story

Built from the Ground Up Since 2018

Rena Farm was founded in 2018 by Nathan Kobaai and Lucy Resiato along Kenya Marble Quarry Road in Kajiado Central — a region well-suited to mixed agribusiness. What started as a determined vision for sustainable, integrated farming has grown into a thriving operation producing cattle, sheep, goats, poultry, fodder, and dairy products.

"Cultivating Sustainability, Enriching Lives through Farming."

Owned and operated by Rena Global Merchants Ltd, the farm is a family enterprise committed to quality at every stage — from breeding selection and animal nutrition, to fodder production and customer delivery. Today, Rena Farm employs 14 dedicated team members and serves buyers across Kenya.

Our Journey

A Farm Built Year by Year

2018

Founded

Nathan Kobaai and Lucy Resiato establish Rena Farm along Kenya Marble Quarry Road, Kajiado Central.

2019–2020

Livestock Expansion

Introduction of Pure Dorper sheep breeding program and Gala goat production. Sahiwaal and dairy cattle added.

2021

Fodder Programme

40 acres allocated to fodder production — Boma Rhodes, Napier, Lucerne, Bracharia and silage operations begin.

2022–2023

Infrastructure Growth

Water pan, drip irrigation, expanded pens, hay barn, CCTV security, and solar lighting installed.

2024–Present

Scaling Up

Poultry enterprise launched. Technology integration, digital marketing, and export market development underway.

Mission & Values

What Drives Us

Mission

To produce high-quality livestock, fodder, and dairy in a sustainable, integrated system that creates real value for our markets and livelihoods for our community.

Vision

To become the leading integrated farm enterprise in Kajiado County — recognised for quality, innovation, and impact.

Sustainability

We grow our own feed, harvest our own water, and manage our land responsibly for future generations.

Integrity

Honest pricing, reliable supply, and genuine relationships with every buyer, partner, and community member.
