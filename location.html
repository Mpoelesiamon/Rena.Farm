Find Rena Farm

Located along Kenya Marble Quarry Road, Kajiado Central — approximately 2 hours 20 minutes from Nairobi CBD.

Nairobi CBD — Start here
Head south on Mombasa Road / A109 towards Mlolongo
Join A104 Nairobi–Namanga Hwy at Mlolongo / Athi River junction
Continue through Kitengela → Isinya → Kajiado Town
Past Kajiado Town, turn at Mla Chake Shop onto Kenya Marble Quarry Road (6.5km off A104)
Rena Farm — 6.5km along Marble Quarry Road, on your right

Tip: Call us before you travel — +254 724 980 372 — and we'll guide you the final stretch to the farm gate.
