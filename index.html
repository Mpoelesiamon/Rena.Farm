Our Story

Founded in 2018 by Nathan & Lucy

Rena Farm was established in 2018 by Nathan Kobaai and Lucy Resiato along Kenya Marble Quarry Road in Kajiado Central — a region naturally suited to mixed farming. What began as a vision for sustainable agriculture has grown into a fully integrated operation producing livestock, fodder, dairy, and poultry.


Owned and operated by Rena Global Merchants Ltd, the farm employs a dedicated team of 14 professionals committed to quality, animal welfare, and sustainable farming practices.

Why Choose Us

What Sets Rena Farm Apart

Pure Genetics

Our Dorper sheep and Gala goats are carefully sourced for superior genetics, growth rates, and meat quality.

Own Fodder Production

We grow our own silage, hay, napier and lucerne — ensuring consistent, high-quality nutrition for all animals.

Water Secure

Borehole, drip irrigation, and a 1M m³ water pan ensure year-round production through dry seasons.

Delivery Available

We deliver bulk orders across the region. Small orders can be collected directly from the farm.

Full Vet Care

On-farm veterinarian ensures all animals are healthy, vaccinated, and meet quality standards at every stage.

Trusted Since 2018

Over 6 years of reliable supply to butcheries, exporters, breeders, and individual buyers across Kenya.
