How to Buy

A simple, transparent purchasing process — from first enquiry to delivery at your doorstep.

Purchasing Process

4 Simple Steps to Buy From Rena Farm

1

Browse & Send an Enquiry

Find your product of interest — livestock, fodder, dairy, or poultry. Contact us via WhatsApp, phone, or the enquiry form with the details of what you need.

2

Visit the Farm (Recommended)

We strongly encourage a farm visit before purchasing livestock — especially breeding stock. See the animals firsthand, assess condition and conformation, and meet our team.

3

Confirm Your Order

Once you are happy with your selection, confirm your order with our team. We will agree on quantities, price, delivery date, and location. A deposit may be required to hold your order.

4

Delivery or Collection

For bulk and reasonable order sizes, we deliver to your location using our farm transport. For small orders, we kindly request self-collection at the farm.

Delivery Policy

Delivery & Collection

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to visit the farm before buying? ⌄
A visit is not strictly required, but strongly recommended for livestock purchases — especially breeding stock. For fodder, most buyers confirm via phone and we arrange delivery.
What is the minimum order for delivery? ⌄
We deliver for bulk orders where the delivery cost is reasonable relative to the product value. Small individual orders are best collected directly from the farm. Contact us and we will advise based on your specific order.
How far do you deliver? ⌄
We deliver throughout Kajiado County and to Nairobi and surrounding areas. For longer distances, contact us and we will quote a delivery cost based on the order size and distance.
Are the animals health-certified? ⌄
Yes. All our animals are on structured health programs including vaccination, deworming, dipping, and regular vet checks. Health records and documentation are available on request.
Can I place a standing order for regular fodder supply? ⌄
Yes — we welcome regular supply arrangements for hay, napier, and silage. Contact our sales team to discuss volumes, frequency, and pricing for standing orders.
Do you export livestock internationally? ⌄
We work with licensed mutton exporters and can facilitate export-grade livestock supply. Contact us to discuss your export requirements.
